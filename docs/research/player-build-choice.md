# Player choice alongside recommendations

All three talent specializations remain selectable in Dungeons, Leveling, Raids
and PvP. Each spec offers all complete, preferred-source catalog allocations via
Other builds, rather than hiding allocations when their original context differs.
Selection persists per class/spec while changing activity. Recommendations are
shown separately and do not overwrite a player's explicit choice.

When no activity-specific guide exists, the page can still show a sourced
reference for that spec. Such a fallback is marked Your choice and explains its
target level and lack of validation for the current activity. For example,
Survival Hunter's raid/tournament allocations can be inspected while Leveling
or Dungeons is selected; this is not presented as a newly validated leveling
route or dungeon optimization. Archived duel allocations retain their extra-point
requirements, and conflicting guides retain their warning.

The banner distinguishes the highest listed damage reference in the fixed
level-60 raid benchmark from a guide pick. Where no comparison establishes a
winner, it says so. It does not compare healing, threat and damage as one score.
The underlying catalog still has source gaps; this change preserves freedom to
play and inspect a spec rather than manufacturing missing activity evidence.
